Campus Dining announced the return of the Flex Meal Deal for fall 2026. Vanderbilt faculty, staff and graduate and professional students can purchase Flex Meals in blocks of 10 and receive three bonus meals at no additional charge during the promotion. The promotion is now live and runs through Saturday, September 12.
