Looking for a new way to partner with Nashville organizations while enhancing your work at Vanderbilt? Applications are now open for the Community Engagement Collaboration Fund, which supports joint projects that involve Vanderbilt faculty and staff and Nashville-based community organizations. Launched in 2024 by the Division of Government and Community Relations, the fund helps create and sustain mutually beneficial partnerships that connect campus and community expertise for new or expanded collaborations.
