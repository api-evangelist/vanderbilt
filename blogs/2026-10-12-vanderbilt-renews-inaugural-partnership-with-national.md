The long-standing relationship between Vanderbilt and the National Museum of African American Music is entering its next chapter with a new three-year partnership agreement. The renewed partnership continues Vanderbilt’s financial support of the museum and builds on the academic and community collaboration that began before the museum opened its doors in 2021.
