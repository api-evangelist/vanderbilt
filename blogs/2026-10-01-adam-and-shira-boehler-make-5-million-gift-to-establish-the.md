Adam and Shira Boehler have made a $5 million founding gift to establish the Boehler Program in Healthcare Entrepreneurship and Innovation, an integrated academic program anchored at the Owen Graduate School of Management designed to advance Vanderbilt’s leadership in healthcare entrepreneurship and innovation education. Known as HCEi, the program will combine Vanderbilt’s strengths in business, medicine, technology and policy within the uniquely dynamic healthcare ecosystem of Nashville—home to the largest concentration of healthcare companies in the United States.
