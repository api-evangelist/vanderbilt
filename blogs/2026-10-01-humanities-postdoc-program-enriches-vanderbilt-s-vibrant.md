Today’s biggest pressing and complex challenges are most effectively addressed through interdisciplinary approaches and perspectives. The Collaborative Humanities Postdoctoral Program fully embodies that approach, bringing together early-career scholars to address important contemporary issues through a critical humanities lens. For the past five years, the program has offered a deeply supportive community for postdoctoral scholars from leading graduate programs nationwide, preparing them to change the future of humanistic study in their respective fields.
