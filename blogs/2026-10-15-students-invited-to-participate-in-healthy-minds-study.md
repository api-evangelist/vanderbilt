Vanderbilt is inviting all undergraduate, graduate and professional students to participate in the Healthy Minds Study, a confidential survey about student mental health and well-being, health behaviors, and use of campus health services. What we learn will help us better understand how students are doing and improve how we work to strengthen the student experience.
