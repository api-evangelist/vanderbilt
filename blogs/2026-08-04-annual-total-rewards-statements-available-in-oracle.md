Your fiscal year 2026 Total Rewards Statement (also known as a Total Compensation Statement) from People, Culture and Belonging is now available. This statement goes beyond your normal pay slip and base pay to show a complete picture of your total compensation; it highlights benefits, retirement contributions, paid time off and more to give you a clear, personalized view of the full value of your employment from July 2025 through June 2026.
