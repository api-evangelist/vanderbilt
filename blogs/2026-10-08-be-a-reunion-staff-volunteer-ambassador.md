Vanderbilt alumni are coming home for Reunion 2026, October 15–18, and we need Vanderbilt staff to help give them a warm Commodore welcome! Sign up to be a Reunion Staff Volunteer Ambassador and help alumni navigate campus, connect with fellow Commodores, and make the most of their Reunion experience. Opportunities include greeting alumni, assisting with registration, scanning nametags, and more.
