The Rohr Chabad House and Lubeck Center for Jewish Life at Vanderbilt University hosted a family weekend Shabbat Dinner on Sept. 18. As part of the celebration, the Live Long & Prosper Tribute Award was presented to Vanderbilt and Chabad leaders in recognition of their commitment to making the world a better place, whether through scientific advancement, community service, autism advocacy or promoting diversity and inclusion.
